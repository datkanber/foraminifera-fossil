Preparation outputs for blinded expert review of LLM-drafted observations

The preparation pilot produced 10 morphology-only LLM_DRAFT records from 10 coded, previously evaluated image cases, comprising 210 character-level entries. The provider-returned model identifier was gemini-3.5-flash-lite. Draft observation states comprised 133 PRESENT, 41 ABSENT, 23 NOT_OBSERVABLE and 13 UNCERTAIN entries. These are unverified model-assigned states, not estimates of observation correctness or diagnostic performance. The coded images and frozen controlled vocabulary were the only specimen information supplied to the model; reference labels, publication identity, previous predictions and scoring outputs were withheld. Raw provider responses, model metadata, prompt and image hashes, and timestamps were retained.

For the two observer roles, 20 pending review instruments were prepared. Both roles receive the identical immutable raw draft for each case, with independent review decisions, edits, timing and explicit approval recorded separately. Actual approved expert forms and independently verified references are currently absent. F001–F060 remain unassigned reservations for 60 new source-documented fossils across the three wall-composition modules. The pilot image cases do not constitute an independent confirmatory test. Consequently, no A0/B1 performance table, accuracy difference or confidence interval is reported at this stage.

The supplied 150-panel S1–S9 archive remains identified as the V27 historical evaluation package and is distinct from the separately hash-verified frozen V29 scoring snapshot. No source manuscript was edited; the methods and preparation-output texts are supplied separately for author integration.
