INTRODUCTION
Six new species of Alveolina have been described in the
specimens collected from the south of Polatlı (SW ANKA-
RA), (figure 1).
The stratigraphy of the south of Polatlı region is given
by the present author (Sirel, 1975).
The specimens are deposited at the paleontologdcal sec-
tion of Mineral Resarch and Exploration Institute of Turkey,
Ankara.
